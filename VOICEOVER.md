# Nicotine: app overview video, voiceover script

**Length:** about 2½ minutes at a relaxed pace (~130 words a minute).
**Tone:** friendly, honest, like talking to a friend over tea. Calm, no hard sell.
**Language:** Bangla–English mix, written in Roman letters so it's easy to read out loud.
**Format:** screen recording of the app + your voice. Optional: your face for the intro and the ending.

How to read this: **[ON SCREEN]** is what to show, the plain lines are what to say.
`(pause)` = a short breath. Words in *italics* = a little emphasis.

### Packs and prices

### Lungs
**[ON SCREEN]** Lungs tab: the breathing lung, the load number, the CO countdown, "If you stop now".

> Eta amar favourite part. Lungs tab.

## Recording tips

- **Record the voice first**, then cut the screen recording to fit it. It's much easier than talking over a fixed video.
- Use the phone's own screen recorder at full resolution. Turn on **Do not disturb** so no notifications pop up.
- Use a **demo profile** with believable data (the design persona: 12 a day, goal 10, 7 today). Don't show real personal data.
- Avoid saying or close-ups of real brand names and pack art. Facebook and YouTube are strict about tobacco ads, and a brand-free video is less likely to be flagged or blocked from boosting. In the script above, no brand is named on purpose.
- For a Reel or Short (9:16, under 60 s), use only sections **1, 2 (shortened), 4 "One tap", 5 and 7**.
- Read slowly. The pauses do half the work.
