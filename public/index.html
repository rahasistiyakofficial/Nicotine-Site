What it does

Honest numbers, in one tap.

Built around how people really buy cigarettes here: one stick at a time, or a pack, at prices that keep changing.

One tap to log

Tap + and it's counted, with the right price. Hold it to pick another brand. Undo is there for five seconds.

Packs and singles

Prices that remember

Each cigarette keeps the price it had when you logged it. Change a price from now, from tomorrow, or from a past date.

Your lungs, as an estimate

A lung picture that shifts with your lung load, a countdown until carbon monoxide clears, and what changes if you stop now. Based on WHO and published research.

Just track, cut down to a daily limit, or quit by a date. Past the limit you get one plain sentence and a one-minute breathing exercise if you want it.

Install

Ready in about a minute.

Nicotine isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it from the file. Here's how.

  1. Download

    Tap Download for Android on your phone. The file is called nicotine.apk.

  2. Open the file

    Open it from the download notification, or from the Downloads folder.

  3. Allow the install

    If Android asks, turn on Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back.

  4. Answer nine questions

    Your age, how many a day, your brand and your goal. Then tap + whenever you smoke.

Google Play Protect may say it doesn't recognise the app. That's normal for apps installed outside the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There's no account and no server, so nobody else can see it, including me. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your logs are gone.

Does it need the internet?

No. Every feature works offline.

Is this medical advice?

No. The lung picture and health numbers are illustrative estimates based on WHO and published research, and the app lists every source. It is not a medical assessment. For help quitting, talk to a doctor.

Can it help me quit?

It can track a quit date, show how long you've been smoke-free, and show health milestones as they pass. It won't lecture you, and a slip isn't a restart.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Android only for now.

My brand isn't listed.

Add it in a few seconds: name, single price, pack price, and a photo if you like. You can change any price later.
